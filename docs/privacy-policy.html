Renegade

Privacy Policy

Renegade is a two-team trivia game. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and how it is stored.

What We Collect

Renegade collects the minimum data required to make the game work across sessions:

  • Anonymous session ID — When you first open the app, an anonymous account is created automatically via Supabase Auth. No email address, name, or password is required or stored.
  • Completed game records — After each game, the app saves the team names you entered, scores, winning team, and which categories were played. This is used to show stats on the home screen.
  • Seen question IDs — The app records which questions you have already been shown so you do not see the same question twice.
  • Timer preference — Your chosen timer setting (30, 45, or 60 seconds) is saved locally on your device only.

What We Do Not Collect

  • No real names, email addresses, or passwords
  • No location data
  • No contacts or address book access
  • No camera or microphone access
  • No payment or billing information
  • No advertising identifiers or third-party tracking

How Data Is Stored

Game data and seen question IDs are stored on servers provided by Supabase, a third-party backend service. Data is associated with your anonymous session ID, not your identity. Supabase's privacy policy is available at supabase.com/privacy.

Timer settings are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage and never leave your device.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties for marketing or advertising purposes. The only third party with access to your data is Supabase, which processes it solely to provide the app's functionality.

Data Deletion

You can clear all your game data at any time from the Settings screen inside the app. This removes your seen question history and game records from our servers.

To request full account deletion, contact us at the email address below.

Children

Renegade is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date below. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
