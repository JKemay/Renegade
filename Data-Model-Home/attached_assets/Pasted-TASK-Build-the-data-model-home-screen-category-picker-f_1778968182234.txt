interface Question {
  id: string;
  categoryId: string;
  tier: 200 | 400 | 600;
  prompt: string;
  answer: string;
  acceptableAnswers?: string[];
  explanation?: string;
  source?: string;

  id: string;
  name: string;
  culture: "circassian" | "jordanian" | "arabic" | "american" 
    | "islamic" | "universal";

  | "split" 
  | "steal" 
  | "phone" 
  | "double" 
  | "veto" 
  | "insider";

  team2Name: string;
  team1Categories: string[];
  team2Categories: string[];
  team1Aids: AidId[];
  team2Aids: AidId[];
  numPlayers: number;
}

5. CATEGORY PICKER — app/create-game/categories.tsx:

Rules:
- Team 1 picks first, then Team 2, alternating until each 
  has 3. Pick order: T1, T2, T1, T2, T1, T2.
- Tapping a card claims it for the current picking team.
- Tapping an already-claimed card by the current team 
  unclaims it (only if total picks < 6).
- Once 6 total are claimed, no more changes.
- "Continue" button only enables when T1 has exactly 3 
  and T2 has exactly 3.
- On Continue: write GameConfig to context and 
  AsyncStorage, then navigate to /create-game/teams 
  (which does not exist yet — the 404 is expected).

UI:
- Header shows whose turn it is: "Team 1 — pick a category" 
  or "Team 2 — pick a category" or "All done" when 6 claimed.
- 2-column grid of category cards on phone.
- Each card shows: name, one-line description, culture tag 
  (small label: "Circassian", "Jordanian", "Arabic", 
  "American", "Islamic", "Universal").
- Unclaimed cards: neutral style.
- Team 1 claimed: distinct color A.
- Team 2 claimed: distinct color B.
- Use 2 colors that are readable on both light and dark 
  backgrounds — your choice, but they must be visually distinct.
