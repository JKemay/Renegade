How it works

🗺️

Pick Your Battlefield

Each team picks 3 topic categories — and also picks the opponent's 3. You decide what they're up against.

⚡

3 Aids, Used Once

Choose 3 power-ups before the game: Skip, Steal, Double, Split, Phone, Veto, or Insider. Each one is gone after you use it.

🔢

6×3 Board

36 tiles. Three tiers (200 / 400 / 600 pts). Teams alternate picking tiles — the harder the question, the higher the stakes.

⏱️

Shot Clock

30 seconds to answer. Configure it to 45 or 60 if you want a bit more room. Wrong answers cost you points.

📚

100+ Categories

From Anime and Hip-Hop to Byzantine History and Entomology. Thousands of questions, tracked so you never see the same one twice.

🏆

No Mercy

Clear the board. Score more. Win. No filler, no fluff — just two teams going head-to-head.
